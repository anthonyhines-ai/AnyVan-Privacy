## 4. Why the calls were hard to find

Five compounding reasons — any one of them makes a naïve search come back empty.

### 4.1 The number is stored as `+447867337470`, not `07867337470`
Twilio and HubSpot hold the number in **E.164** (`+44…`), and HubSpot even holds spacing/trailing-space
variants (`+447867 337470`, `+447867337470 `). A literal search for `07867337470` or `07867 337470`
matches **nothing**. You must normalise to the **last 10 digits** (`7867337470`) — the repo rule.

### 4.2 The raw call→listing mapping is empty for these calls
`TWILIO_CALL_TO_LISTING_MAPPING` returns **0 rows** for all 36 SIDs. Any tool, workflow or dashboard
that pulls "calls for this booking" **through that table** shows nothing — which looks exactly like
"there are no calls." The listing link *does* exist, but only in `FACT_VOICE_ACTIVITY` (populated from
the Flex TaskRouter reservation), not in the raw mapping table. Use the former.

### 4.3 Freshdesk is not the system of record for telephony
The calls live in **Twilio (audio) + HubSpot (engagement) + the warehouse** — not in Freshdesk. The
warehouse `FRESHDESK_TICKET` table is empty, and there is no ticket↔call join. An agent sitting inside
ticket `2264362` will not see the calls unless they cross-reference the customer's number in HubSpot or
the warehouse.

### 4.4 The calls are scattered across ~10 queues and 3 teams
Handled through **AVC_Sales_Priority / AVC_Sales_Inbound, CS_Inbound, CS_On_the_Day,
[en_GB]_CS_OTD/Future/Complete_Priority**, plus outbound and "Everyone" manual dials — spread across
**Inbound (Sales), Customer Service and On-The-Day** teams and **25+ different agents**. Anyone looking
in a single queue or team view sees only a slice.

### 4.5 Two bookings + a quote, and no transcripts
The activity splits across **quote `25718241`**, **booking `9269436`** and **booking `9298817`**, so a
single-booking view is incomplete. And every call is a **Twilio Flex call = audio only**: there are
**no machine transcripts** (`EVENTS_CALL_TRANSCRIPTIONS`/`JIMINNY_CALL_TRANSCRIPT` return nothing for
Flex). Anyone hunting for a *text* record of what was said will find none — only the `.mp3`.

## 8. Methodology / learning (reusable)

Phone key = **last 10 digits** (`07867337470` → `7867337470`). `"FROM"`/`"TO"` are reserved words —
double-quote them. Reuse the channel map in `booking-lookups/METHODOLOGY-communication-history.md`.

```sql
-- Raw call legs
SELECT ID, DIRECTION, STATUS, "FROM", "TO", START_TIME, END_TIME, DURATION
FROM HARMONISED.PRODUCTION.TWILIO_CALL
WHERE RIGHT(REGEXP_REPLACE(COALESCE("FROM",''),'[^0-9]',''),10)='7867337470'
   OR RIGHT(REGEXP_REPLACE(COALESCE("TO",''),'[^0-9]',''),10)='7867337470'
ORDER BY START_TIME;

-- Agent-attributed + listing-linked (the one-stop table for "who dealt with this")
SELECT EVENTTIMESTAMP, VOICETYPE, TASKQUEUENAME, WORKERFULLNAME, WORKERTEAM,
       LISTING_ID, ANSWERED, TALKTIME
FROM MART_SALES_OPS.PRODUCTION.FACT_VOICE_ACTIVITY
WHERE RIGHT(REGEXP_REPLACE(COALESCE(CUSTOMERPHONENUMBER,''),'[^0-9]',''),10)='7867337470'
ORDER BY EVENTTIMESTAMP;
```

Recordings are retrieved from HubSpot `CALL` engagements associated with the contact
(`hs_call_recording_url`), **not** from Snowflake — the warehouse holds metadata, HubSpot/Twilio hold
the audio. **The metadata outlives the audio** (see §10): a call can be fully evidenced in the
warehouse long after its recording has been purged.

**Key lesson:** when someone says "we can't find the calls," the failure is almost always one of
§4.1 (E.164 vs `0…`) or §4.2 (missing raw listing link) — check both before concluding there are none.
If the *audio* specifically is missing, check the call date against the retention rule (§10) before
assuming a fault.

## 10. Recording availability under the retention policy

Call-recording **audio** is governed by AnyVan's retention policy, which **changed on 5 May 2026**:
**3 months** before that date, **12 months** from it. Retention affects the **audio only** — the call
*metadata* (that the call happened, when, direction, duration, agent, queue, talk time) lives in the
warehouse and the HubSpot engagement and is **not** deleted by this policy.

**Mechanism — how the change interacts with existing recordings.** A recording is purged once it
exceeds the window in force, and a purge cannot be undone; extending the window only preserves
recordings *still alive* at the switch. So a pre-5-May-2026 recording survives to today only if it was
**less than 3 months old on 5 May 2026** — i.e. the call was on/after **~5 Feb 2026**. Anything earlier
had already been deleted under the 3-month rule before the window extended.
